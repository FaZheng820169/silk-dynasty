What we sell

Silk Dynasty produces AI-generated portraits of you, wearing historically researched Chinese robes. They are images, not photographs. Every file we deliver is labelled as AI-generated and we will never describe it as a photograph.

Placing an order

  • You choose a set and send a photograph. There are two ways to sit: Automatic, where a renderer makes one free proof frame from your photograph, and Concierge, where we look at the photograph by email and art-direct the set ourselves.
  • In Concierge mode, if the photograph will not work — hidden hands, heavy shadow, a filter — we say so and ask for another. Nothing is charged until we both agree to proceed.
  • Prices are in US dollars and shown before you pay.

The free proof

An Automatic sitting renders one proof frame from your own photograph before you pay anything. It exists so you can judge the quality of the work, and you may keep it. It is a preview, not part of what you buy: it is a single frame, it may carry a visible mark, and it is not covered by the redo promise below.

Delivery

The set you pay for is delivered by email within 48 hours of payment, usually sooner:

  • Single Robe — four portraits, 2K, personal licence.
  • Dynasty Trio — nine portraits across all three dynasties, 4K, with styling notes.
  • Heirloom — the nine Trio portraits at 4K, plus a 24-page album printed and shipped within 10 working days. International shipping times vary.

Redo

If a set misses — the face is not you, the body has changed, the hands are wrong — tell us within 14 days of delivery and we will rebuild it once at no cost. That one rebuild is the limit of the guarantee.

Refunds

Because the work is made to order, we do not refund a delivered set — apart from the free redo above. If we fail to deliver at all, you are refunded in full. Nothing here removes rights you have under the consumer law of your country.

Your licence

You receive a personal, non-exclusive, worldwide licence to use your portraits for anything personal: printing, sharing, social media, gifts. Commercial use — advertising, merchandise, resale — needs our written permission, because the garment and styling work is ours.

What we ask of you

  • Send a photograph of yourself, or of someone who has given you clear permission.
  • Do not send photographs of children without being their parent or guardian.
  • Do not ask us to depict a real public figure, or to place anyone in a defamatory, sexual or unlawful scene.
  • You confirm you are 18 or older, or that a parent or guardian is placing the order.

We may decline or cancel an order that breaks these rules, and will refund any payment made.

Our limits

We do not warrant that a generated portrait will be a perfect likeness — generative models vary, and that is inherent to the medium. We are not liable for indirect losses, and our total liability for any order is limited to the amount you paid for it.
