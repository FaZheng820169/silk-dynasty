Privacy

Last updated 25 October 2026

We ask for one of the most personal things a person can send: a photograph of their body. This page says exactly what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Your photograph. Only when you choose to send it to us. On the studio page it stays in your browser until you attach it to an email.
  • Your email address, so we can quote, deliver and follow up on your order.
  • Your name and any notes you add — both optional.
  • Order details: the set, the dynasty, the amount paid, the delivery date.

What we do with it

  • We use your photograph to make the portraits you ordered, and for nothing else.
  • We do not use it to train, fine-tune or evaluate any model.
  • We do not sell it, publish it, or show it to anyone outside the people finishing your set.
  • We do not use it in advertising, on this site, or on social media.
Your photograph is deleted 30 days after delivery, or immediately if you ask us to — whichever comes first. Delivered portraits are kept for 12 months so you can download them again, then removed.

Who sees it

A small number of service providers process data on our behalf: our email provider, our payment processor, the GPU renderer that turns your photograph into the first proof frame, and — for Heirloom orders — the print house that produces your album. Each is bound to use the data only to perform that service. We do not share your photograph with any of them except the renderer (only when you choose Automatic delivery, and only for that one frame) and the print house (only for the frames that appear in your album).

If you choose Concierge delivery, your photograph is never sent to a renderer — it reaches us by email and is worked on by a person.

Legal bases

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on contract (to produce and deliver what you ordered) and consent (for the photograph you choose to send). You can withdraw consent at any time by asking us to delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Write to hello@silkdynasty.studio and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority; in California, you have the rights set out in the CCPA/CPRA, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them.

Cookies and measurement

This site sets no advertising cookies and runs no third-party tracking scripts. If we add privacy-friendly traffic measurement later, it will be cookieless and aggregate only, and this page will say so.

Children

The service is for adults. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must place the order and send the photograph on your behalf.
